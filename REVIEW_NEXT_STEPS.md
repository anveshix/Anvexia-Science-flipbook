## Teacher/science reviewer checklist

1. Review each image at full zoom against the relevant syllabus and trustworthy scientific sources. Confirm or correct every English/Hindi label, description and leader-line target. Record whether essential features are missing; replace or redraw misleading imagery rather than approving it by appearance alone.
2. Treat AI-generated scenes as conceptual drafts, not photographs, measured data or functioning experiments. Check anatomy, historical apparatus, molecular models, wiring and safety. In particular, the movable-pulley rope and distillation apparatus are unverified; do not use them as assembly or safety instructions. Static images do not show motion, experiments, or slider responses.
3. Check Cell division/meiosis, membrane and tissue diagrams for stage, molecular geometry and anatomy; illustrations are not microscopy. Check Rutherford detector geometry and the oxygen/water teaching props; they are not confirmed historical apparatus or molecular photographs.
4. Verify all Grade IX/X lesson text and calculations independently. Known issues requiring owner-authorized review include X 1.9 rancidity wording, 2.5 pH range, 5.5 ATP yield, 8.4 independent assortment, and 13.4 biomagnification. Some atomic sliders allow fractional values.
5. Validate all 288 requested concepts against an authoritative list. A spread may contain multiple concepts, and some concepts may need more than one visual or dynamic interaction. Obtain user approval for replacements and uncertain placeholder mappings.
6. Test screen-reader use, keyboard, touch, all chapters and modes, browsers, actual GPUs and classroom projector readability before production. DOM overlay labels might not appear in canvas exports. Keep all items pending until independently checked and signed off.
