# Tissues and Motion — offline draft review

Extract the entire ZIP and open `TISSUES_MOTION_REVIEW.html` in a browser. Click an image to enlarge; Escape closes the lightbox. It uses local images only. The 17 images correspond to Tissues 3.1–3.11 and Motion 4.1–4.6; topic titles come from the application checklist.

These are unapproved AI-generated conceptual illustrations, 1408 × 768 pixels, not real photographs or micrographs. They have not been scientifically certified or teacher-approved. Headings are not anchored English/Hindi labels. All images require subject and language review. Many show only one part of a larger topic; review notes below each draft flag known gaps. In particular, still images do not establish speed or acceleration, and a properly scaled, reviewed distance–time graph must be added separately to topic 4.6. Tissue 3.8 needs multiple connective-tissue types, 3.9 needs other muscle types, and 3.11 needs injury and nutrition content.

The live application is unchanged. None of these 17 drafts are integrated into the main or staging app. This is a review package, not the full 266-spread rollout. `ROLLOUT_STATUS.csv` records progress across all topics.
